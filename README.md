## About

I build and operate production software end to end: schema design, cloud
infrastructure, API, web and mobile.

My main work is a **B2B SaaS platform for industrial construction companies** — a
Turborepo monorepo of twelve applications (web, admin, landing, mobile, a voice
gateway and five background workers) that pulls data out of ERP APIs, normalises it
into a single model across multiple databases, and makes it answerable through
LLM-powered agents. I am responsible for its architecture, its Terraform-provisioned
AWS infrastructure, its delivery pipeline and its production operation.

I also build and publish **Firmo90**, a habit-recovery app on the App Store and
Google Play. Solo, over the past year: React Native and Expo on the client, Express
and PostgreSQL behind it, with subscriptions, push, realtime, localisation and
release automation to the stores.
